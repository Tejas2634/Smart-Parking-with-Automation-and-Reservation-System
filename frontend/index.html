Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I enter the parking ground?

Drive up to the entrance gate. The Raspberry Pi ANPR Camera will read your license plate (e.g. MH 19 BJ 1234) and automatically lift the barrier arm.

2. Can I pre-book my slot before arriving?

Yes! Go to the "Pre-Book" tab, select your arrival time, vehicle plate, and duration. You'll receive a digital QR ticket instantly.

3. What if I stay longer than my pre-booked time?

Overtime is calculated automatically at checkout (₹50/h for cars, ₹30/h for bikes) with no penalty fees.

Raspberry Pi Edge Barrier Integration

Pi Camera Module Rev 1.3 (OV5647 CSI) + SG90 / MG996R Servo Motor

1. Pi Camera Rev 1.3 (CSI Port)

Insert the 15-pin flat ribbon cable into the CAMERA (CAM) port. Ensure the blue backing tape faces the Ethernet/USB ports and metal pins face the HDMI port.

2. SG90 Servo Motor (Barrier Boom)

Brown/Black: Pin 6 (GND) | Red: Pin 2 or 4 (5V Power) | Orange/Yellow (PWM Signal): Pin 12 (GPIO 18).
